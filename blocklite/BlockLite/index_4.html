About BlockLite

Publications:
BlockLite: A Lightweight Emulator for Public Blockchains with High Flexibility and Scalability, SIGMOD19 (under review).

Introduction:
BlockLite is an easy-to-use and lightweight emulator. BlockLite is designed to be deployed to a single node, and can control the block generation time at a fine granularity. Also, users are able to quickly emulate the execution of their new blockchain protocols or applications at a large scale.
