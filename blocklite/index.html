BlockLite
  • A lightweight and programmable blockchain emulator
Summary

BlockLite is a lightweight and programmable emulator for public blockchains. BlockLite is designed to be deployed to a single machine and the block generation time is controlled at a fine granularity. Users are able to quickly emulate the execution of their new blockchain protocols or applications at a large scale, e.g., tens of thousands of blockchain nodes. The following three figures show BlockLite's real-time executions (memory footprint) on 5,000, 10,000, and 20,000 nodes, respectively. The test bed has 48 AMD EPYC cores and 192 GB memory. All experiments take in one million transactions. The proof-of-work difficulty is set to one, as per the definition in the paper.
