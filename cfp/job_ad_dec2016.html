Computer Science PhD Positions at University of Nevada, Reno (UNR), United States

The High Performance Data Intensive Computing (HPDIC) laboratory at UNR has multiple openings of PhD students starting in Spring/Fall 2017. Full financial support (tuition waiver, monthly salary ranging $1,700 - $2,500 depending on experience) will be provided through Research and/or Teaching Assistantship. Lab members will have opportunities to collaborate (e.g., internship) with our industry partners (e.g., IBM, Google, Amazon) and government labs (e.g., Pacific Northwest National Lab, Lawrence Berkeley National Lab, Texas Advanced Computing Center). More information of the lab can be found here: https://www.cse.unr.edu/hpdic

Interested applicants should email a single PDF file to Prof. Dongfang Zhao at dzhao@unr.edu with:

  • Statement of Motivation (one page)
  • Curriculum Vitae, or Resume (max. five pages)
  • One representative publication, if any (English only)

Founded October 12, 1874, UNR is the flagship and sole land grant institution for the state of Nevada. Nevada borders Oregon to the northwest, Idaho to the northeast, California to the west, Arizona to the southeast and Utah to the east. Reno is located in Northern Nevada, approximately a half hour's drive from Lake Tahoe, two hours from Sacramento, and three hours from San Francisco.
