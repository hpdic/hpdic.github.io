I am an assistant professor in the Computer Science and Engineering department at the University of Nevada at Reno. I obtained my Ph.D. degree in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) under the guidance of Prof. Ravi Iyer and Prof. Zbigniew Kalbarczyk.'

My research interests span multiple disciplines in computer science/engineering and electrical engineering. These disciplines include cybersecurity, Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), Internet of Things (IoT), Software-Defined Networking (SDN), operating systems, virtual machines, and power system analysis. My primary interests are on cybersecurity, especially in the context of Cyber-Physical Systems and Internet of Things, such as smart grids.
